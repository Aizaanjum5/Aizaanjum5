### 🔬 Research & Ongoing Work

- 🩺 **Algorithmic Detection of Dependency Conflicts in Medical Workflows** *(Talent UD / DETEP)* Investigating graph-based algorithmic frameworks to identify and resolve dependency conflicts and constraint violations in complex, multi-step medical treatment protocols.
- 🦷 **Deep Learning for Early Detection of Dental Caries** — Developing deep learning and computer vision architectures (CNNs) for automatic feature extraction, data augmentation, and classification of early-stage dental caries from diagnostic imagery.
- 📦 **How Bug Fixes Propagate in the NPM Ecosystem** *(Co-Author, Under Review)* Conducted empirical software engineering research at Nicolaus Copernicus University (TSSP) analyzing how bug fixes propagate through complex, multi-tiered software package ecosystems by parsing dependency graphs.
